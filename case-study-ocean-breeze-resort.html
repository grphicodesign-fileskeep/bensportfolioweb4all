The Challenge

Repositioning La Union Hospitality: From Weekend Surfers to High-Value Long-Stay Nomads

San Juan, La Union has traditionally been seen as a weekend party and surfing getaway. Ocean Breeze Resort sought to redefine this paradigm by positioning itself as an exclusive, design-led long-stay sanctuary catered to remote executives, creative digital nomads, and wellness seekers seeking weekly and monthly beachfront residencies.

The challenge required solving two distinct problems simultaneously: creating a sophisticated, editorial brand identity with luxury physical posters that inspire travelers to stay longer, paired with a custom web application capable of handling complex reservation workflows without saddling the owner with expensive hotel software subscription fees.

Challenge 01

Perception & Positioning

Overcoming the transient, crowded budget-hostel aesthetic prevalent across surf towns by introducing an elevated architectural and minimalist editorial visual narrative.

Challenge 02

Zero-Overhead Automation

Architecting a custom reservation database connecting directly to Google Sheets and Apps Script, eliminating $150+/month property management subscriptions while offering real-time date verification.

Print Advertising Campaign

Architectural Fine-Art Posters: Celebrating Coastal Serenity & Modern Surf Culture

To build physical brand presence across regional airports, boutique coffee shops, and resort galleries, I developed a collection of luxury promotional posters. Designed with Swiss-style typographic discipline, high-contrast serif headlines, and cinematic natural photography, the posters celebrate both the legendary Pacific golden hour and the resort's brutalist-tropical architectural elegance.

Print Spec 01

Typographic Hierarchy

Pairing refined classical display serifs with crisp, open-tracking geometric sans for timeless legibility from distance in hospitality lobbies and galleries.

Print Spec 02

Museum-Grade Calibration

CMYK color spaces with deep contrast ratios calibrated for archival textured cotton paper and minimalist matte black aluminum framing.

Brand Identity System

Natural Color Harmony & Tactile Brand Touchpoints

The brand identity palette draws inspiration from the natural elements of the San Juan coast: sunset terracotta, sun-bleached sand cream, deep Philippine sea navy, and coastal jade. The visual language extends into tactile stationery, blind-embossed stationery envelopes, business cards, and a cohesive mobile digital identity.

Color Token 01

Terracotta Orange (#F25B0E)

Infused into primary action buttons, status accents, and sunset visual cues to inspire warmth and hospitality vitality.

Color Token 02

Acts as the foundational background canvas, replacing harsh digital white with organic, calming beach resort texture.

Color Token 03

Deep Navy & Charcoal (#0F172A)

Supplies razor-sharp typographic contrast, luxury glassmorphic card overlays, and high-impact structural borders.

Color Token 04

Coastal Jade Accents

Represents coastal flora, tropical gardens, and the pristine surf waters of Northern Luzon.

Web Platform Engineering

Full-Stack Web Experience: High-Speed Vite, Motion & Real-Time Booking Verification

The Ocean Breeze Resort digital portal was developed using modern React 19, TypeScript, Vite, and Motion. The application features zero page reloads, fluid view transitions, and dedicated sections covering Accommodations, Long-Stay Lifestyle Amenities, Guest Story Archives, Interactive Google Maps turn-by-turn routing, and an intelligent date-check reservation engine.

Feature 01

Interactive Calendar Date Lock

The booking modal performs live availability queries directly against the master spreadsheet, visually slashing out booked dates and preventing double bookings in real time.

Feature 02

Turn-by-Turn La Union Navigation

Direct GPS deep-linking launching Google Maps, Waze, or Apple Maps with single-click arrival coordinates at Urbiztondo, San Juan.

Feature 03

Offloaded Web Worker Sanitization

Input sanitization and payload formatting are handled off the main UI thread via background Web Workers, maintaining a butter-smooth 60fps frame rate.

Feature 04

Long-Stay Pricing Engine

Intelligent tier calculations applying automated 15% weekly and 30% monthly stay discounts for nomad and extended residency guests.

Automation Infrastructure

Serverless Automation Hub: Google Sheets as a Real-Time Reservation Database

Rather than forcing the resort management team into complex backends or paying hefty monthly subscriptions to enterprise hotel software, the platform routes bookings into Google Sheets via Google Apps Script Webhooks.

Whenever a guest reserves a room, the webhook sanitizes the submission, logs a formatted row with a unique tracking ID, updates calendar availability, and immediately dispatches multi-channel alerts (via Twilio SMS, Facebook Messenger API, and backup HTML Gmail notices) directly to the resort manager's phone.

Pipeline 01

Two-Way Sheet Synchronization

Staff can edit approval states directly in Google Sheets (e.g. changing status to "Confirmed" or "Checked In"), and the live web app reflects updates instantly.

Pipeline 02

Instant Multi-Channel Alerts

Direct integration hooks to Twilio SMS and Facebook Messenger Graph API notify the front desk within seconds of reservation dispatch.
